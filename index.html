My Favorite Characters from Vinland Saga
Thorfinn
A broken boy who grows into a man searching for peace. His journey inspires many fans.
Hild
A fierce, skilled huntress carrying deep pain. Her strength and heart make her unforgettable.
Thors
A warrior who chose kindness over violence. He’s the father figure everyone admires.
Askeladd
A clever, unpredictable leader with a hidden heart. Loved for his depth and complexity.

If you haven’t watched Vinland Saga yet, you’re missing out on something special. Start with the anime — Season 1 (24 eps) and Season 2 (24 eps) — then jump into the manga from chapter 100 to 220. I honestly think this is one of the greatest pieces of fiction ever made. It’s deep, philosophical, emotional, and it completely shifted the way I look at life and people. Makoto Yukimura poured real meaning into this story, and the lessons inside hit harder the more you think about them. Give it a chance… it might change you into a True Warrior.
